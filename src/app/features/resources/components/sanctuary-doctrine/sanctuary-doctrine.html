Confidential Diplomatic Mechanism

The Neutral Sovereign Sanctuary Doctrine

By joint bilateral decree of West African signatory ministries, negotiations convened under The Baobab Group's Track 1.5 auspices operate within an inviolable diplomatic perimeter. No proceedings, participant identities, or territorial concessions may be subpoenaed by domestic judicial bodies or non-regional tribunals.

🛡️ Non-Disclosure & Immunity Standard (Dakar Framework · Art. 22)

All customary mediators and accredited sovereign envoys enjoy total diplomatic transit immunity and complete record privilege within participating ECOWAS states.
