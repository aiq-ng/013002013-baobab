We provide an ancestral sanctuary for sovereign dialogue and restorative justice.

The Baobab Group acts as West Africa's trusted Track 1.5 diplomatic mediator. Rooted in century-old customary governance and reinforced by contemporary security intelligence, our missions establish safe neutral grounds where state sovereigns, regional ministries, and armed non-state factions resolve underlying land and water disputes.

By shifting the strategic security architecture away from punitive isolation toward sovereign dignity and communal reintegration, we dismantle radicalization pipelines before crises spill across sovereign borders.
